Simple Blog Typography Example

By John Doe · November 2025

This is a simple blog page demonstrating clean and readable typography. The layout is basic and uses internal CSS to keep things easy for beginners.

Why Typography Matters

Good typography makes content easy to read. It also improves the overall look and feel of a blog page.

“Typography is the art of making written language readable and beautiful.”

Basic Rules to Follow

  • Use readable fonts
  • Keep line spacing comfortable
  • Use proper heading structure
  • Add enough white space
